Privacy Policy

Scan, create, and share QR codes & barcodes securely.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Overview

Scanora is developed as an offline-first utility application. Your privacy is paramount: Scanora does not require user accounts, does not collect personal identity information, and does not upload your scanned or generated barcodes/QR codes to external servers.

2. Device Permissions & Data Access

To provide core functionalities, Scanora requests specific device permissions:

  • Camera: Used exclusively in real-time to scan QR codes and barcodes. Camera frames are processed locally on your device and are never recorded, saved, or transmitted to any server.
  • Photo Library / Storage: Used only when you choose to scan an image from your gallery or save an exported QR/barcode image to your device.

On-Device Processing: All scanning algorithms, text recognition, and barcode generation run locally using your device's native hardware and APIs.

3. Data Collection and Tracking

Scanora does not collect, store, sell, or rent your personal data. We do not track your activity across third-party apps or websites, and we do not use third-party analytics SDKs that profile users.

4. Third-Party Links & Content

When you scan a QR code or barcode containing a web link (URL), opening that link will launch your device’s default web browser or app. Those external websites operate under their own independent privacy policies.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.
