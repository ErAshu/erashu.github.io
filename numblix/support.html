# Numblix Support We’re here to help! If you have any questions, need assistance, want to report a problem, or have feedback about **Numblix**, please feel free to contact us. ## Contact Us For support and assistance, please contact us at: **Email:** mobileappbucket@gmail.com We will do our best to respond as soon as possible. ## Report a Problem If you experience an issue while using **Numblix**, please include the following information when contacting us: * App name and version * Your device model * Your iOS or Android version * A detailed description of the issue * Steps to reproduce the problem, if possible * Screenshots or a screen recording, if available This information helps us investigate and resolve issues more quickly. ## Feedback and Suggestions We value your feedback and are always looking for ways to improve **Numblix**. If you have a feature request, suggestion, or idea, please send it to us at **mobileappbucket@gmail.com**. ## Frequently Asked Questions ### How do I report a bug? Please send us an email at **mobileappbucket@gmail.com** with details about the problem. If possible, include screenshots or a screen recording. ### I found a problem in the app. What should I do? Contact our support team and describe the issue. Please include your device model and operating system version to help us investigate. ### Can I suggest a new feature? Yes! We welcome feature suggestions. Please send your ideas to **mobileappbucket@gmail.com**. ### How can I get help with the app? Simply contact us at **mobileappbucket@gmail.com**, and we will do our best to assist you. ## Privacy Policy Please review our Privacy Policy to learn more about how information is handled when you use **Numblix**.
